The banner displays your welcome message and a copyable server address. Use the showcases to introduce your game modes or community activities.

Activate Unruly in **Administration → Themes**. Open **Configure** to choose the accent color, replace the banner and showcase illustrations, or link your launcher and Discord server.

Unruly was created by Orphevs and Eywek and adapted for KraftInk. [Original MineWeb theme](https://github.com/MineWeb/Theme-Unruly)
